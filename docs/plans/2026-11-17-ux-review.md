# The 7" dashboard: a review for clarity (2026-11-17)

The owner, 2026-11-17: "too many places where things are just thrown and the buttons and such seem to blend in." This note
is what a review of the dashboard found: what this PR changes now (small, safe), and what needs her call first, with a
recommendation for each. The review used the dashboard test's screenshots at 1024×600 (the 7") and the CSS and JS.

## Changed in this PR

- **DUMP and DISK no longer look disabled.** With no T48 or no store they were faded to 40 %, the same look as a disabled
  button, though both tabs open. Now only the name goes quiet (the dim text colour, 4.8:1 on the panel); the status line
  under it says why.
- **The one action a card is for stands out.** `button.primary` had only a magenta border, the one thing separating it from
  the plain buttons around it. It now has a magenta fill as well (text stays 11:1).
- **SHUT DOWN reads as the dangerous hold.** It had red text; it now has the red border as well, so it doesn't look like a
  twin of HOLD TO EXIT KIOSK beside it.
- **A disabled button shows a "not allowed" cursor** (phone and mouse), and a button or link reached by keyboard shows a cyan
  outline. The 7" is touch only and never sees either.
- **"not logging" beside STOP LOGGING.** With logging on and no readings, the METER line said "not logging" next to a
  STOP LOGGING button. It now says "waiting for readings" (the first reading starts a file), which is what the logger does.
- **SYSTEM → Storage said "dumps: not there yet (M7)"** when the dumps folder doesn't exist. M7 has been done since
  2026-09-29; it now says the folder is made with the first dump.

## For the owner to decide

**1. DISK: a finished job pushes the controls off the screen.** After a READ or an IMPORT, its result card (STORED,
IMPORTED, VERIFIED…) sits above the mode buttons and the drive list, so on the 7" the drive list starts half-way down and
step 2 is below the fold. *Recommended:* the result shrinks to one line ("IMPORTED carnevil-hdd_… · DETAILS") once she
taps anything else or after a minute, with DETAILS bringing the full card back.

**2. DISK: seven mode buttons in one row** (READ, WRITE, FORMAT, SWAP, RECORDS, FILES, CHECKLIST), all the same weight.
Three of them change a drive, two only look, and SWAP and CHECKLIST are both the bench procedure. *Recommended:* two
groups with a gap between them: READ · WRITE · FORMAT, then FILES · RECORDS · SWAP & CHECKLIST (one button, the checklist
as SWAP's second half).

**3. Toasts cover the header.** A toast ("+5V confirmed…", "IMPORT queued…") sits over the clock and the logger status for
a few seconds. *Recommended:* toasts at the bottom of the screen, above nothing she needs at that moment.

**4. The METER's dial-mode chip shows a bare "—" with no reading,** a small cyan pill left of the profile button that
reads as a stray dash. *Recommended:* hide it until a reading names the mode, or say "NO MODE".

**5. Help on each tab** (her next-phase feature 12) answers a lot of "what do I press first": one line under each tab's
title. Best written by her, in her words; the code is small.

Each of 1 to 4 is a small change on its own. Say which to do, and in what form.
